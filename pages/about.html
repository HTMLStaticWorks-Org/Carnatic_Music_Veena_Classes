Custodians of the Thanjavur Saraswati Veena Lineage

Founded on Vijayadashami 1988 in Mylapore, Chennai, Nadam Veena Academy bridges centuries-old courtly Vainika tradition with rigorous modern conservatory pedagogy.

Our Origin Story (1988) மயிலாப்பூர் பாரம்பரியம்

From a Mylapore Agraharam Veranda to a Global Classical Conservatory

In October 1988, Vidushi Dr. Meenakshi Sundaram began teaching seven young disciples on the red-oxide courtyard of her ancestral home on North Mada Street, Mylapore, within earshot of the Kapaleeshwarar Temple bells. Her vision was clear: the Saraswati Veena must never be reduced to a mechanical plucked lute; it must sing every syllable of Tyagaraja and Dikshitar with the breath and inflection of the human voice.

Over nearly four decades, that humble veranda blossomed into a full-fledged conservatory housing acoustic practice chambers, a dedicated Thanjavur jackwood veena repair and fret-setting studio, an archival palm-leaf manuscript library, and high-definition dual-camera online classrooms serving 1,450+ students worldwide.

Unbroken Sampradaya குரு பரம்பரை வம்சாவளி

The Guru-Shishya Parampara Lineage Tree

Every student enrolled at Nadam inherits an unbroken chain of musical transmission tracing directly to the architects of the modern twenty-four-fret Saraswati Veena.

I
17th Century • Thanjavur

Raghunatha Nayak & Govinda Dikshitar

Standardized the 24 fixed brass-fret (melam) Raghunatha Mela Veena in the royal court of Thanjavur, enabling all 72 Melakarta ragas to be played on a single instrument.

II
18th–19th Century

Muthuswami Dikshitar Vainika Sampradaya

The "Vainika Gayaka" of the Carnatic Trinity infused deep dhrupad-inspired gamakas, madhyamakala passages, and temple philosophical iconography into veena technique.

III
Early 20th Century

Thanjavur & Karaikudi Bani Veterans

Preserved the majestic Ghanaraga Tanam plucking patterns, unhurried vilambita kala phrasing, and disciplined right-hand thala-string coordination.

IV
1988 – Present

Nadam Veena Academy Acharyas

Led by Vidushi Dr. Meenakshi Sundaram and seven senior faculty members transmitting this living heritage to the fifth generation of global disciples.

Our Artistic Credo நாத யோகத்தின் நான்கு தூண்கள்

Mission & The Four Pillars of Our Pedagogy

Pillar 01 • Sruti Mata Laya Pita

Uncompromising Sruti Alignment

“Sruti is the Mother and Laya is the Father.” Before a student plucks a single phrase, we train the ear to merge effortlessly with the acoustic overtones of the Shadjam-Panchamam-Shadjam drone.

Pillar 02 • Gayaki Bani

Singing Through the Strings

Every veena student learns the full vocal Sahitya (lyrics) of a Geetham, Varnam, or Kriti first, ensuring that right-hand meettu strokes mirror natural syllabic articulation rather than arbitrary plucking.

Pillar 03 • Lakshya & Lakshana

PracticeWedded to Musicology

We pair practical finger dexterity (Lakshya) with clear theoretical grammar (Lakshana)—understanding why a raga uses a specific vakra phrase, jiva swara, or nyasa resting note.

Pillar 04 • Seva & Craftsmanship

Direct Patronage of Thanjavur Viswakarma Artisans

Every instrument acquired through our academy directly supports fourth-generation veena carvers in Thanjavur and Bobbili who season Panasa (jackfruit) wood for up to seven years.
