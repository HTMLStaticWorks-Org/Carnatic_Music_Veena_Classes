Privacy Policy & Student Data Stewardship

Effective Date: 1 November 2026 • Nadam Veena Academy, Mylapore, Chennai

1. Information We Collect from Disciples & Parents

When you register for a course, book a free demo class, or reserve a concert pass at Nadam Veena Academy, we collect your full name, email address, phone/WhatsApp number, time zone, chosen musical discipline, and current Carnatic proficiency level. For Bala Nada students under age 18, registration is completed with verifiable parent or guardian consent.

2. Online Dual-Camera Class Recordings

To support home sadhana, 1-on-1 and group online sessions may be recorded for educational review. Recordings are stored privately inside the enrolled student’s encrypted portal and are never shared publicly or on social media without explicit written authorization from the student or parent.

3. Payment Security (UPI, Stripe & PayPal)

Nadam Veena Academy does not store raw credit/debit card numbers or bank credentials on our servers. All tuition transactions are processed directly via PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant gateways (Stripe, PayPal, and NPCI BHIM UPI).

4. Local Browser Storage (Theme, RTL & Sadhana Diary)

Our website uses browser localStorage strictly to remember your Dark/Light theme preference, Right-to-Left (RTL) layout setting, Tamil/English heading toggle, and practice notes inside the Student Dashboard.

5. Contacting Our Data Protection Officer

To request a copy, correction, or deletion of your student records, write to privacy@nadamveena.org or visit the Secretariat at No. 42, North Mada Street, Mylapore, Chennai — 600004.
