15th Melakarta • Agni Chakra

Raga Mayamalavagowla

Aro: S R1 G3 M1 P D1 N3 Ṡ
Ava: Ṡ N3 D1 P M1 G3 R1 S

The foundational dawn scale codified by Purandaradasa for all beginner Sarali, Janta, and Alankaram exercises due to its symmetrical dual-tetrachord intervals.

28th Janya • Audava-Audava

Raga Mohanam

Aro: S R2 G3 P D2 Ṡ
Ava: Ṡ D2 P G3 R2 S

Universally auspicious pentatonic raga omitting Madhyamam and Nishadam. Home to Vara Veena Geetham, Ninnukori Varnam, and Nanupalimpa.

65th Melakarta • Rudra Chakra

Raga Mecha Kalyani

Aro: S R2 G3 M2 P D2 N3 Ṡ
Ava: Ṡ N3 D2 P M2 G3 R2 S

Foremost Prati Madhyama raga of grandeur and evening radiance. Every swara is a Gamaka-rich Kampita resting point on the Saraswati Veena.

29th Melakarta • Bana Chakra

Raga Dheera Shankarabharanam

Aro: S R2 G3 M1 P D2 N3 Ṡ
Ava: Ṡ N3 D2 P M1 G3 R2 S

The "Ornament of Lord Shiva"—majestic Sarva-Swara Gamaka Varika Rakti raga ideal for expansive Ghanaraga Tanam and Raga Tanam Pallavi.

29th Janya • Audava Scale

Raga Hamsadhwani

Aro: S R2 G3 P N3 Ṡ
Ava: Ṡ N3 P G3 R2 S

Created by Ramaswami Dikshitar and immortalized by Muthuswami Dikshitar’s "Vatapi Ganapatim Bhaje"—traditional concert opener.

8th Melakarta • Netra Chakra

Raga Hanumatodi (Todi)

Aro: S R1 G2 M1 P D1 N2 Ṡ
Ava: Ṡ N2 D1 P M1 G2 R1 S

Profound karuna and bhakti raga demanding extraordinary left-hand finger control on Gandharam and Dhaivatam deflections.

Essential Discography

Recommended Archival Veena Listening

To internalize authentic raga swaroopa, our Acharyas recommend listening daily to these five landmark Vainika recordings:

  • Karaikudi Sambasiva Iyer (1952 AIR Archive) — Unmatched authority in Ghanaraga Tanam and Ata Tala Varnams.
  • Vainika Vidwan S. Balachander — Deep multi-swara Jaru deflections and monumental Raga Bhairavi & Todi alapanas.
  • Thanjavur K.P. Sivanandam & Sarada Sivanandam — Purest vocalized Thanjavur bani duet phrasing.
  • Vidushi E. Gayathri & Dr. Jayanthi Kumaresh — Contemporary clarity of tone, symmetrical tanam, and dynamic laya dialog.
  • R. Pichumani Iyer & Chitti Babu — Lyrical tonal sweetness and intricate bell-like cascade techniques.

Saraswati Veena Care & Melam Maintenance Guide

A handcrafted jackfruit-wood Saraswati Veena is a living organic resonator that responds to temperature, humidity, and skin oils. Follow these four rules to preserve its sruti for decades:

  1. 1. Protecting the Beeswax Melam (Fret Ledge): Never expose the veena to direct sunlight or car trunks; heat softens the beeswax-and-charcoal compound and shifts the 24 brass frets out of 22-sruti alignment.
  2. 2. Wiping Steel & Brass Strings After Sadhana: Wipe the 4 main playing strings and 3 side thala strings with a dry mulmul cloth after every practice session, applying a single drop of pure coconut or mineral oil monthly to prevent oxidation.
  3. 3. Caring for the Kudam & Surakkai Gourd: Always rest the main jackwood Kudam on a padded velvet ring or folded jamakkalam mat—never directly on cold stone or tile floors.
  4. 4. Seasonal Peg (Kunti) Seasoning: During dry winter months or in heated Western homes, rub a pinch of dry chalk dust on wooden pegs that slip, or pure beeswax on pegs that bind.
