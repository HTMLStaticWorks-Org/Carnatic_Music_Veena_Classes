Frequently Asked Questions by Students & Parents

Grouped by Admissions, Online Classes, Veena Instrument Selection, Grade Exams, and Tuition Fees.

FAQ Topics and Search

For Carnatic Vocal, children may begin at age 5.5 to 6 in our Bala Nada starter batch. For the Saraswati Veena and Classical Violin, we recommend starting at age 7 (using a half-size or three-quarter Bala Veena so the child’s left arm comfortably reaches the first fret without shoulder tension). There is no upper age limit—many of our most dedicated students began in their 40s, 50s, and 60s.

No prior musical training is required. Because our academy follows the Thanjavur Gayaki Bani, our teachers automatically teach you to sing each Sarali Varisai, Geetham, and Varnam phrase vocally alongside playing it on the strings.

Our Acharyas broadcast from dedicated studio rooms equipped with two synchronized camera feeds: Camera 1 shows a top-down close-up of the left hand’s fret deflections, while Camera 2 captures the right hand’s index/middle meettu and thumb thala-string strokes. On your end, a standard laptop or tablet positioned at a 45-degree angle with "Original Sound for Musicians" enabled is all you need.

Yes. Morning IST slots (6:30 AM – 9:30 AM IST) align comfortably with evening hours across North America (EST/CST/PST), while afternoon/evening IST slots (3:30 PM – 8:30 PM IST) serve the UK, Europe, Middle East, Singapore, and Australia.

If you attend in-person classes at our Mylapore campus, you may use our resident Thanjavur veenas during class and rent a practice veena for home use (₹750–₹900/mo). For online students across India or abroad, our luthier desk sources, seasons, and tunes custom Thanjavur Ekantha or Ottu jackwood veenas packed in airline-grade fiberglass cases and ships door-to-door via DHL/FedEx air cargo to the USA, UK, Europe, and Australia.

In the authentic Thanjavur Gayaki tradition taught at Nadam, we pluck with the natural pad and slight fingernail tip of the index and middle fingers to produce a warm, vocal, skin-on-string tone. For students with soft nails or concert projection needs, we also train in custom-fitted brass/silver wire nakhams during advanced Tanam modules.

We conduct annual internal jury examinations across four tiers (Prarambha, Madhyama, Praveena, and Vidwat) and also prepare students for Tamil Nadu Music & Fine Arts University, Prayag Sangeet Samiti, and Trinity Carnatic grade exams. A solo Veena or Vocal Arangetram is typically undertaken after completing the Praveena level (usually 5–7 years of dedicated sadhana), when the student can render Varnams, Pancharatna Kritis, Raga Alapana, Tanam, and Kalpanaswaram independently.

Absolutely. Online disciples complete live proctored practical juries via dual-camera video and receive physical embossed certificates by courier. Whenever our NRI students visit Chennai during December (Margazhi) or summer holidays, they are invited to perform on our Mylapore Sabha stage.

For 1-on-1 plans, students may reschedule up to 2 classes per month directly inside the Student Dashboard as long as notice is given at least 24 hours before the scheduled slot. For group batches, an HD cloud recording and annotated notation PDF of the session are automatically posted to your dashboard.

Yes! A flat 15% Sibling/Family Discount applies when two or more members of the same family enroll, and quarterly/annual payments carry 10% and 20% savings respectively. We also award six full-tuition Nada Vidya Scholarships every Vijayadashami to meritorious young vainikas from economically modest backgrounds.
