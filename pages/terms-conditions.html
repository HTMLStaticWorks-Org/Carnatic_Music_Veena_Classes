Gurukulam Terms, Attendance & Code of Conduct

Last Updated: 1 November 2026 • Governing Mylapore Campus & Global Online Enrollment

1. Guru-Shishya Etiquette & Punctuality

Students are expected to join their online Dual-Camera studio or Mylapore classroom 5 minutes prior to the scheduled time with their Saraswati Veena, Violin, or Mridangam tuned to their assigned kattai (sruti).

2. 24-Hour Class Rescheduling Policy

Students enrolled in 1-on-1 plans may reschedule up to 2 sessions per calendar month through the Student Dashboard, provided the request is logged at least 24 hours before the scheduled slot. Unannounced absences (no-shows) count as a completed session.

3. Intellectual Property of Archival Notations

All swara-sahitya PDFs, meettu-annotated Tana Varnam sheets, and audio reference tracks inside the Student Dashboard are the copyrighted scholarly property of Nadam Veena Academy and are licensed solely for the enrolled student’s personal sadhana.

4. Mylapore Campus & Rented Instrument Care

Students utilizing academy jackwood veenas or renting home instruments agree to keep the beeswax melam protected from heat, direct sunlight, and impact. Any structural damage beyond normal string wear is covered by the refundable instrument deposit.

5. Tuition Billing & Cancellation

Monthly tuition is billed on the 1st of each month. Students wishing to pause or discontinue enrollment may do so before the 25th of the preceding month with zero penalty.
