Saraswati Veena: Foundation to Tana Varnam Mastery

Our flagship conservatory program led by Vidushi Dr. Meenakshi Sundaram and Vidwan S. Raghavan Iyer. Available in 1-on-1 Online (Dual-Camera) and Mylapore Campus batches.

8 Graded Modules 2 Sessions / Week (60 mins each) University & Sabha Certified

This comprehensive curriculum guides a student from their very first seated posture and open-string Shadjam-Panchamam tuning through the complete Purandaradasa Abhyasa Gana framework into rich, concert-grade Adi Tala Tana Varnams and foundational Kritis.

Step-by-Step Curriculum

8-Module Conservatory Syllabus

Understanding the Kudam (resonator), Dandi (neck), 24 brass frets (melam), Yali headpiece, and Surakkai gourd. Ergonomic cross-legged posture, right-hand index (Tarjani) and middle (Madhyama) alternate plucking, and thumb strokes on the three side Thala strings (Sa–Pa–Sa).

Left-hand index and middle finger placement across the 15th Melakarta scale (S R1 G3 M1 P D1 N3 Ṡ). Practicing all 14 Sarali Varisais in three geometric speeds (Prathama, Dwitiya, and Tritiya Kala) synchronized with Adi Tala thala-string strokes.

Developing double-note Spuritam gamaka strength through 12 Janta Varisais (SS RR GG MM), upper-octave Tara Sthayi reach up to Madhyamam, and zig-zag Dattu Varisai leaps that build fret-board spatial memory.

Mastering Chatusra Jati Dhruva (14 beats), Matya (10 beats), Rupaka (6 beats), Misra Jati Jhampa (10 beats), Tisra Jati Triputa (7 beats), Khanda Jati Ata (14 beats), and Chatusra Jati Eka (4 beats) talas on the side rhythm strings.

Transitioning from pure swara exercises to Sahitya-based melodies: Sri Gananatha, Kundagowra, Kereya Neeranu, and Padumanabha in Raga Malahari, followed by Vara Veena (Mohanam), Kamalajadala (Kalyani), and Janakasutha (Saveri).

Learning Bilahari and Kalyani Jatiswarams alongside Syama Sastri’s monumental Raga Khamas (Sambasiva Dayanidhe) and Bilahari (Rara Venu Gopa Bala) Swarajathis with introductory Kampita and Jaru gamakas.

In-depth study of Ninnukori (Mohanam), Evvari Bodhana (Abhogi), Vanajakshi (Kalyani), and Jalajaksha (Hamsadhwani) in two speeds, paired with foundational Kritis including Vatapi Ganapatim and Maha Ganapathim.

Every student learns independent peg tuning (kunti), micro-adjusting the brass beads (mani), caring for beeswax frets, and performing a 25-minute graded practical recital accompanied by a live mridangam artist.

Measurable Learning Outcomes

Play all 7 Suladi Sapta Talas while simultaneously plucking the three side thala strings on Laghu and Dhrutam beats.

Articulate Sahitya syllables using authentic Gayaki meettu rules (plucking only on new syllables, deflecting within vowel extensions).

Execute Kampita, Nokku, Spuritam, and Ravai gamakas cleanly across 12 major Melakarta and Janya ragas.

Tune all 7 strings of the Saraswati Veena by ear to an electronic or acoustic tambura within 3 minutes.
