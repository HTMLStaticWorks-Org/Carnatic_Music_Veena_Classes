Vidushi Dr. Meenakshi Sundaram

Founder-Principal, Nadam Veena Academy • All India Radio 'Top Grade' Vainika • Ph.D. in Carnatic Musicology (University of Madras)

38 Years Teaching 96+ Arangetrams Mentored Thanjavur Gayaki Bani

Artistic Biography & Sampradaya

Born into a family of Sanskrit scholars and vainikas in Tiruvaiyaru, Dr. Meenakshi Sundaram underwent rigorous gurukulavasam for over two decades under Vidwan Thanjavur K.P. Sivanandam’s senior disciples. Her Doctoral dissertation at the University of Madras examined the microtonal 22-sruti deflections of the Kampita and Jaru gamakas in Muthuswami Dikshitar’s Kamalamba Navavaranam kritis.

Known for her unhurried, deeply meditative Ghanaraga Tanam and ability to coax three full octaves of resonance without contact microphones, she has performed at the Madras Music Academy, Shanmukhananda Hall (Mumbai), Théâtre de la Ville (Paris), and the Cleveland Tyagaraja Festival.

Sabha Honours & Academic Credentials

  • Sangita Kala Acharya (2019) — Conferred for excellence in preserving classical veena pedagogy.
  • Vainika Sikhamani (2014) — Sri Krishna Gana Sabha, Chennai.
  • All India Radio 'Top Grade' Artist (since 2006) — Featured regularly in National Programme of Music broadcasts.
  • Author of "Tantri Nada Dipika" (2018) — 420-page treatise with swara-meettu notations for 35 Adi and Ata Tala Tana Varnams.
